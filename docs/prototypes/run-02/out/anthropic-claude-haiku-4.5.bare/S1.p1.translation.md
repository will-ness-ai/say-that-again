## Q5 — Confirmation: which directions confirm, and what do they say?

Step back. The two directions each have a risk, both handled before this point:

- **Open (→ public):** all verified members gain the ability to read the full message history. Once you open it, closing it later does not erase what people already read. Ticket #109 asked whether the open path warns about this.
- **Close (→ private):** all verified members without the composite role lose the channel right now. Auto-grant (#111) kept the people who posted. People who only read get no warning. The ticket asks whether the command names that number.

What the code gives us: the bot caches all members in the gateway. `/pillar-list` already counts role holders from that cache. Both counts are fast:

- Open: verified members who gain access = verified count minus composite-role count.
- Close: verified members who lose access = the same number, for the same reason.

Why this matters:

- A confirmation step stops a slip of the finger. `visibility` is one choice on one command — `public` and `private` are one click apart.
- If the state you ask for already exists, skip the confirmation. The command just reports the current state.
- When you change all channels at once (Q2), confirm once for the whole batch, not once per channel.

I suggest: **confirm both directions with buttons that disappear, and show the count in both.**

- Open: "This makes the full message history of #x readable by ~N verified members. A later close does not undo that. Continue?"
- Close: "~N verified members lose access to #x. They get no notification. Continue?"
- Skip confirmation if the state already matches — just report, and fix any drift you find.
- One confirmation covers all channels in the batch, with the channel names in the message.
- Buttons vanish after 60 seconds. Timeout means cancel.
