**Q5 — Confirmation: Which directions confirm, and what do they say?**

Look at the big picture. The two directions have different risks. Both were handled earlier:

*   **Open (→ public):** The channel gives `read_message_history` to all verified members. The entire private history can be read immediately. Closing it later does not take back access. People read it while it was open. Ticket #109 asked if the open path warns about this. This ticket answers that.

*   **Close (→ private):** Every verified member without the special role loses the channel immediately. Auto-grant (#111) protected people who posted. Pure readers lose access with no warning. The ticket asks if the command names show this.

Facts from the code: The bot keeps all members in its gateway cache. `/pillar-list` already counts role holders from that cache. So, both counts are fast:

*   **Open:** How many verified members get access = (total verified members) - (members with the special role).
*   **Close:** How many members lose access = the same number, for the same reason.
