**Q5 — Confirmation: which directions confirm, and what do they say?**

The two directions carry different risks, both already decided upstream:

- **Open (→ public):** all verified members gain `read_message_history`. The full private history becomes readable at once. A later close does not take it back — people read it while open. Ticket #109 asked whether the open path warns about this.
- **Close (→ private):** every verified member without the composite role loses the channel at once. Auto-grant (#111) protected people who posted. Pure readers lose it with no alert. Ticket #111 asks if the command names that number.

The code already has what you need: the bot caches all members in the gateway. `/pillar-list` counts role holders from that cache. Both counts are fast:

- Open: verified members who gain access = verified count minus composite-role count.
- Close: verified members who lose access = the same number.

Why confirm:

- A confirmation step stops a typo. `visibility` is one field on one command — `public` and `private` are one click apart.
- Skip the confirm if the state already matches. No transition means nothing to confirm. The command just reports and fixes drift if it finds any.
- The batch form (Q2) confirms once for all channels, not once per channel.

**My recommendation: confirm both directions with Confirm/Cancel buttons, and show the count in each.**

- Open: "This makes the full message history of #x readable by ~N verified members. A later close does not undo that. Continue?"
- Close: "~N verified members lose access to #x. They get no notification. Continue?"
- No confirm when the state already matches — the command only reports and repairs drift.
- One confirm covers all channels in the batch, with the channel list in the prompt.
- Buttons expire after 60 seconds. Expiry cancels.
